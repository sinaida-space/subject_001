About

The story so far.

Human first. Digital second.

I believe that technology is only meaningful when it helps people feel seen, heard, and connected.

Origin

Biomedical engineering, MSc., Bauman Moscow State Technical University

Drift

Ballet. General Electric IT Leadership Program. Generative systems.

Focus

Real-time visual worlds driven by body tracking and sound.

I build living visual systems for stages, concerts, and performance spaces. They breathe with sound, respond to bodies, and turn light, image, and generative code into a shared atmosphere.

Services

Digital tools for human connection.

Sound

For music festivals & concerts

Audio-reactive stage visuals, one system per song, listening to the live mix straight from the desk. Delivered as a turnkey show or operated live.

Nine projections, one per song, performed live with Redkie Ptitsy at Sklad No. 3, Moscow, March 2026.

Code

For interactive web

Sites and components that respond to the visitor. Real-time WebGL and shaders, camera and gesture control running on-device, generative sound. Delivered as a finished site, or as a single component handed to a team that already has developers.

Every interactive piece on this site was built this way, including Aether Currents and Ethereal Path. All live, all playable now.

Body

For theater & dance

Responsive scenography: real-time systems that follow the performers’ bodies and the sound, developed with the creative team from first concept onward.

The tracking runs live and in public: hands drive sound and image in Aether Currents, head and hands steer the whole scene in Ethereal Path.

Space

For venues, brands & institutions

Immersive installations and generative visual identities, adapted to the space they run in.

Prototyped as interactive projections for The Eyes Chico and CONSPACE ROOMS, Prague 2026. Both web forms are finished and live.
