# The idea behind sinaida.eu

Every atom of a body was made in a star. The site is built on that thought: the stars on the page and the people who meet here are one material, and we are all creatures of the same universe.

So there is one galaxy and one trajectory. The visitor scrolls, and a single flight carries them through the stars from the first screen to the footer. The galaxy delivers everything: the headline breaks into star dust, the belief arrives on its wind, About is poured from it, Body of Work is a sphere made of it, and the offerings turn on a tesseract lit by the same light. Nothing on the home page comes from anywhere outside the galaxy.

What follows from it:

- Stars are connection, between space and body and between people. They are never decoration.
- Space bends. The camera dives into a surface, swings to the side, rises; the trajectory may be strange, but it is one continuous path and it always moves with the scroll.
- Content is never pasted on top of the sky. It condenses out of it and returns to it.
