# Performance pass 261010 (issue #173)

Measured 2026-10-10 on branch `261010_quote-gate`, headless Chrome (playwright-core 1.64, ANGLE Metal) on an Apple M2. CPU slowdown via `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate`: 4x stands in for a 2019 MacBook Air, 6x for a weaker machine. GPU and compositor are not throttled, so real old hardware will be somewhat worse than these numbers on canvas-heavy screens.

## Method

- Build: `vite build` (production mode, without the prerender steps of `npm run build`, which are not needed for runtime numbers), served locally through `vite preview`.
- Scroll: wheel steps of 100 px every 16 ms from the top to the bottom and back (phones: `scrollBy`, mobile UA, touch, 390x844). Frame times come from a rAF probe, long tasks from a `longtask` observer. Sections are assigned by the screen middle: hero/quote gate, About, bang + Work build (About end to the work strip), strip, Services (title + tesseract), Contact.
- Dropped-frame share: missed vsyncs divided by elapsed vsyncs.
- Load: 4x CPU + Fast 4G (165 ms RTT, 9 Mbps down, 1.5 Mbps up), cache off. TBT is the standard sum of (task − 50 ms) after FCP; the plain sum of long tasks is in the raw log. JS is encoded bytes of script responses (the preview server sends them uncompressed, so production with gzip or brotli transfers less). WebGL contexts are counted by wrapping `getContext`.
- Idle: page rAF callbacks in 1 s, after 3 s at rest, at 5 positions.
- Tools: `perf173.mjs`, `prof173.mjs`, `trace173.mjs`, `seam173.mjs` in the scratchpad.

## Headline

- Full mode, desktop 1470: every section holds 60 fps at 1x. At 4x only the bang + Work build stretch is over budget: p95 66.7 → 50 ms after the fix below, 7 → 6 long tasks. At 6x it is 100 → 66.8 ms.
- Full mode, phone 390: p95 16.8 ms in every section even at 6x; the bang has 4 long tasks at 4x (max 133 → 67 ms).
- Lite: p95 ≤ 16.8 ms everywhere at 4x on both widths, TBT 0.
- Load at 4x + Fast 4G: full FCP/LCP ≈ 0.97 s (desktop), 0.80/0.92 s (phone); lite 0.74 s and 0.71/0.79 s. TBT full 400 to 780 ms (bimodal across runs, the same before and after), lite 0. CLS 0 at load and after a full scroll in every case.
- Idle: 0 rAF at rest in full mode at all 5 positions. Lite shows 60 at About only because the lite `DustReveal` divider is still in its one-time reveal 3 s after arriving (it converges in about 5.7 s, then stops).

## What the profile shows in the bang + Work build stretch (1470, 4x)

- `WorkBuild` `sample()` at the start of the bang: one task of about 185 ms (sampling About's text and portrait into grains). Still there.
- Strip cards: all `DitherReveal` frames load together as the strip nears; each decoded its image synchronously in `drawImage` (145 ms of decode in total) and built its frames in the same moment (getImageData, about 180 ms). Fixed: decode off the main thread, build in idle slots.
- `ConstellationFull` `revealDraw`: a canvas-to-canvas `drawImage` per revealing node per frame, about 160 ms over the stretch. Still there.
- Several long tasks are mostly `Commit` (main thread waiting on the compositor, 57 to 132 ms) while the map and the strip come in. Cause not isolated; it may partly be headless compositing.
